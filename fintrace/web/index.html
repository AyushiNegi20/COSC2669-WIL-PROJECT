    A focused research prototype

    Two banks, two financial years and 13 financial measures. Answers use selected pages from six official reports, not every page or every public disclosure.

    Supported: finding figures, compatible year-on-year changes, same-period cash/statutory profit gaps and report excerpts. Currency conversions, calculating new ratios, cross-company rankings and automatic claim verdicts are not enabled.

    Explanations are source excerpts, not generated summaries. Cash profit and cash flow are different concepts. Wording can still be misinterpreted, so review the source before relying on an answer.

    This is not financial advice or an independently certified system. Successful development tests are not a claim of accuracy on unseen questions.
