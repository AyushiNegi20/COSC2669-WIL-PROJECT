    A focused research prototype

    Six official CBA and NAB reports cover FY2024 and FY2025. Prose search includes extractable text across all six reports. You can ask about report content beyond the original thirteen numerical measures. Checked calculations cover those measures, CBA investment spending and selected NAB statutory and reconciliation tables. Other tables and image-only content are not automatically calculation-ready.

    CBA's financial year ends on 30 June. NAB's ends on 30 September. Similar labels do not always mean comparable measures. Explicit cross-bank comparisons are currently declined.

    Checked numbers and arithmetic use source-bound Python code. Other report answers use local Qwen to select cited passages, or checked paraphrasing for definitions. A quotation is not a new calculation or proof of complete coverage. Follow-ups currently support changing the bank or year of the previous question, not unrestricted conversational memory.

    This is a development prototype, not financial advice or independently certified output. Check the source before relying on an answer.
