\subsection{Data sources and scope}
\label{subsec:data_sources}

The knowledge base contains six reports published by CBA and NAB for FY2024 and FY2025: two CBA Profit Announcements, two NAB releases of full year results and two NAB Annual Reports. Together they contain 1,021 PDF pages. A source register records each document's title, issuer URL, reporting period and checksum so that the original files can be identified~\cite{fintraceSources2026}. The results releases bring together performance tables and management commentary~\cite{fintraceCBA2025,nabResults2025}; the NAB annual reports add accounting notes and reconciliations~\cite{nabAnnual2025}.

Banking offered repeated financial measures and authoritative public documents against which answers could be checked. Two years support historical comparisons within a bank. The reporting periods nevertheless require care: CBA's year ends on 30 June and NAB's on 30 September. The same financial year label therefore does not imply identical periods. The prototype excludes comparisons between banks.

Native text, meaning text stored directly in the PDF, was extracted from every report. An initial selection of 72 pages received more detailed table extraction and checking. Prose search was later extended across the full reports. These processes provide different levels of coverage: searchable text is available more widely than values approved for calculations. The original thirteen core measures defined a starting scope rather than an exhaustive list of financial information in the documents.
