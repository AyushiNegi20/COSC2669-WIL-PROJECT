\subsection{Iteration in response to failures}
Development followed a repeated process of asking a question, preserving the response, inspecting the source and identifying the failing stage. Errors could arise during extraction, retrieval, interpretation, matching a financial figure or presentation. This diagnosis determined whether the next change concerned the evidence, routing, calculation or model.

The September extraction work compared table representations and introduced a logged correction for invisible brackets. Investment questions showed that the largest spending category could differ from the category with the greatest annual increase. Comparison questions also revealed the need to identify when an earlier year's value came from a later publication, without automatically describing the difference as a restatement~\cite{fintraceExtraction2026,fintraceCandidate2026}.

The 27~September coverage review separated reading the full reports from approving values for calculations. It added NAB statement and reconciliation checks and repaired paragraphs split across PDF layout blocks. On 4--5~October, wording tests extended general profit and EPS answers to the available labelled alternatives. The 13~October update distinguished a request for an unavailable historical year from a prediction of future profit~\cite{fintraceCoverage2026,fintraceBroad2026,fintraceHistory2026}.

Failed responses were retained as regression cases. Separate AI-assisted assessment sets were kept outside the runtime index and linked to the versions tested. Once an assessment informed a fix, its questions were no longer treated as unseen. Later improvements therefore did not automatically inherit an earlier accuracy result.

\subsection{Mentor feedback and delivery}
Mentor feedback on reasoning and behavioural questions encouraged testing beyond exact metric names. For example, a question about where CBA was investing required distinguishing its own spending from lending activity and checking whether the evidence supported a ranking. Relevant passages alone did not establish a complete answer.

The Sprint~5 retrospective record identifies three priorities: retain failed responses, test realistic wording and stabilise the demonstration rather than continually expand its scope. The record was clarified on 2~October without specifying a retrospective meeting date. Explanation, coverage and response-time improvements remained visible in the \href{https://trello.com/c/6hV8pwiq}{post-demo backlog}.
