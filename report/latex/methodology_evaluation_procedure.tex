\subsection{Evaluation Procedure and Validity Safeguards}
\label{subsec:evaluation_procedure}

The release-assessment procedure records the candidate commit, model configuration and evidence artifacts before running questions. Integrity checks compare runtime and source files with the release manifest before and after assessment. This establishes whether the candidate changed, not whether its answers are financially correct~\cite{fintraceEval2026}.

Questions and reference answers remain separate from runtime evidence. The sanctioned runner uses \texttt{serve\_fintrace\_demo.build\_backend()} so that assessment exercises the configured application rather than generic defaults. Every first response is preserved, including errors and fallbacks, instead of retrying until a favourable response appears. Timing is recorded separately from correctness.

Assessment reads the full response: displayed paragraphs, structured claims, calculations, excerpts and limitations. This avoids an earlier adapter error that missed calculations present in structured output. Claims are checked against cited pages for row identity, year column, unit, reporting basis, operational scope and report vintage. Equivalent units and reasonable rounding can be accepted, but neighbouring measures cannot. For explanations, a correct figure without requested drivers is incomplete.

Source disagreements are investigated before changing the answer or key. Original expectations and failed responses remain alongside corrections. A later report's comparative column is identified by its source version; a difference from an earlier report does not alone establish a formal restatement. Compatible columns matter when computing historical changes~\cite{fintraceCandidate2026}.

Validity is limited by small, purposive sets, AI-assisted judgments and changing coverage. No independent human-panel assessment or controlled analyst study was conducted. Exposed questions cannot provide fresh generalisation estimates after tuning. Generation and latency can vary between runs. Older release results are therefore historical evidence, not scores automatically assigned to the current checkpoint. The report's live frontend screenshots illustrate exposed examples; they are not a fresh assessment or a new accuracy estimate.
